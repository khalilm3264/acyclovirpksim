# AyFlux community code of conduct

AyFlux welcomes constructive contributions from students, educators, researchers, clinicians, and developers.

We expect contributors to:

- Be respectful, patient, and clear when discussing scientific or software disagreements.
- Discuss evidence and technical claims without personal attacks or harassment.
- Consider accessibility and the needs of people with different levels of technical experience.
- Respect privacy, copyright, authorship, and the rights of other contributors.
- Avoid publishing identifiable patient information, credentials, or non-public health data.
- Separate educational simulation from patient-care advice in public discussions.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, or intentional disruption are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, or related project discussions.

If you have a conduct concern, contact **contact@ayflux.org**. Please provide sufficient context without sharing sensitive medical information. The project maintainer may edit, close, or restrict contributions that violate these guidelines.

For code contributions, read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). For vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
