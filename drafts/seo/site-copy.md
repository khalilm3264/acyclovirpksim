## Compact homepage description

AyFlux is an educational pharmacokinetic simulator for acyclovir and valacyclovir. Explore how assumed renal function, medication timing, and dialysis treatment settings influence model-predicted plasma, cerebrospinal fluid (CSF), and CMMG concentrations. All results are simulations, not measurements or patient-specific treatment recommendations.

**Short safety line:** For educational use only. Not for clinical dosing or dialysis prescriptions.

## About AyFlux

AyFlux is an open-source project created by Muhammad Khalil to make pharmacokinetic modeling more accessible to learners. Its browser-based simulator lets visitors explore how changes in kidney function and dialysis assumptions affect simulated acyclovir profiles, including models of plasma, CSF, and the CMMG metabolite.

The tool provides scenarios for intermittent hemodialysis, peritoneal dialysis, CRRT, and no dialysis. It can also illustrate dosing schedules, modeled uncertainty, and how different assumptions change predicted results.

AyFlux is intended for education and exploration of model behavior. Its underlying assumptions are simplified, and outputs have not been established as clinically reliable for individualized decisions. A graph that appears to match a reference study does not demonstrate predictive accuracy for an individual patient. For methods, validation limitations, and primary-source references, see the User guide.
