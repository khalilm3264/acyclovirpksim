### 2. Discrepancies and Typographical Errors in the Publication

1. **Table 1 Flow Rate Units:**
   - Table 1 (p. 352) labels both dialysate flow (`900`) and ultrafiltration rate (`100` and `800`) under column headers with units of `(ml/min)`.
   - Text on page 352 explicitly states: *"dialysate... flow rate of ~ 900 ml/h, and dialysate outflow rate ~ 1000 ml/h"*, and *"dialysate outflow rate... of 1700 ml/hour"*.
   - A dialysate flow of $900\text{ mL/min}$ would equal $54\text{ L/h}$ (standard single-pass intermittent hemodialysis, not continuous therapy) and would yield clearances exceeding $500\text{ mL/min}$. The table header `(ml/min)` is an indisputable typographical error for `ml/h`.

2. **Figure 1 Modality Heading:**
   - Figure 1 (p. 353) labels the second continuous therapy block (spanning $t \approx 124\text{--}148\text{ h}$) as `CVVHD-CA210`.
   - The article title, abstract, Table 1, and text on pages 351, 352, 353, and 354 uniformly designate this session as **CVVHDF** (continuous veno-venous hemodiafiltration), reflecting the large net ultrafiltration of $800\text{ mL/h}$ added to the $900\text{ mL/h}$ dialysate flow ($Q_{\text{out}} = 1700\text{ mL/h}$).

3. **Blood-Side vs. Effluent Extraction Inconsistency:**
   - During CVVHD (F-8), predialyzer plasma ($13\text{ mg/L}$) equals postdialyzer plasma ($13\text{ mg/L}$), giving a blood-side extraction of $0\%$, yet the effluent contains $11\text{ mg/L}$ (effluent saturation $84.6\%$).
   - During CVVHDF (CA-210), predialyzer plasma ($13\text{ mg/L}$) falls to $10\text{ mg/L}$ postdialyzer ($23.1\%$ drop). At $Q_b = 200\text{ mL/min}$ and hematocrit $25.6\%$ ($Q_p = 148.8\text{ mL/min}$), blood-side plasma clearance is $34.3\text{ mL/min}$ (diffusive) or $\sim 45\text{ mL/min}$ with ultrafiltration ($13.3\text{ mL/min}$). This is 2.6 times the effluent clearance of $17.2\text{ mL/min}$.
   - This phenomenon mirrors the blood-side vs. effluent discrepancy reported by Collignon et al. (2023) and confirms that effluent-based calculations provide the only reliable recovery accounting in clinical CRRT.

### 3. Actual Drug Removal vs. 24-Hour Projections

#### Occasion 1 (CVVHD, F-8 Membrane):
- **Documented Event:** The circuit clotted after **6 hours** of operation due to extracorporeal thrombosis (p. 352 col 2).
- **Actual Measured Drug Removed:**
  $$\text{Delivered Effluent Volume} = 6\text{ h} \times 1.0\text{ L/h} = 6.0\text{ L}$$
  $$\text{Actual Drug Mass Extracted} = 6.0\text{ L} \times 11\text{ mg/L} = \mathbf{66\text{ mg}}$$
  $$\text{Fraction of Single 750 mg Dose} = \frac{66\text{ mg}}{750\text{ mg}} = \mathbf{8.8\%}$$
  $$\text{Fraction of Prescribed 1500 mg Daily Dose} = \frac{66\text{ mg}}{1500\text{ mg}} = \mathbf{4.4\%}$$
- **Reported 24-Hour Figure ($264\text{ mg}$, $18\%$):**
  - This is an **extrapolated projection**, calculated as $24\text{ h} \times 1.0\text{ L/h} \times 11\text{ mg/L} = 264\text{ mg}$ ($264 / 1500 = 17.6\% \approx 18\%$).
  - Treating $264\text{ mg}$ or $18\%$ as observed patient recovery would quadruple the true delivered removal ($66\text{ mg}$).

#### Occasion 2 (CVVHDF, CA-210 Membrane):
- **Documented Event:** The circuit ran for approximately 24 hours ($t \approx 124\text{--}148\text{ h}$, Figure 1).
- **Reported 24-Hour Figure ($322\text{ mg}$, $65\%$):**
  - Derived from spot effluent ($7.9\text{ mg/L}$) multiplied by $24\text{ h} \times 1.7\text{ L/h} = 40.8\text{ L}$:
    $$40.8\text{ L} \times 7.9\text{ mg/L} = 322.32\text{ mg} \approx 322\text{ mg}\quad (322 / 500 = 64.5\% \approx 65\%)$$
  - Because spot sampling occurred while plasma concentrations were falling across the interval ($14 \rightarrow 3.7\ \mu\text{g/mL}$ in Figure 1), a true cumulative collection would yield somewhat less than the static $322\text{ mg}$ calculation.
