Oral acyclovir tablets

Explore how much a preceding oral course could contribute to two IV measurements, or compare a single tablet dose with published dialysis cohorts. For educational research.

Tablet absorption and sources

The label reports average oral bioavailability of 10–20%, decreasing with dose. In a crossover study, steady-state peak/trough concentrations were 0.83/0.46, 1.21/0.63 and 1.61/0.83 mg/L at 200, 400 and 800 mg. Each scenario explores a fixed dose with a selectable fraction. The uptake rate is an assumption. US tablet label

Medicated peritoneal dwell

Acyclovir starts in the first dialysis bag. Change when that bag drains to see how much drug remains in it and how much reaches the circulation.

Burgess and Gill used 1 g intraperitoneally, 1.5 L exchanges with 1.5% dextrose and six-hour drains. Observed systemic bioavailability was 61 ± 10%. Boelaert’s separate IV-based model projected 91% for hypothetical peritoneal administration. Burgess and Gill · Boelaert et al.

Simulation assumptions

Body weight is the reported CAPD group mean of 68.3 kg. Residual creatinine clearance is assumed zero. Fresh bags follow every six hours after the first drain. Relative exposure divides 240-hour plasma AUC after the medicated fill by AUC after an equal IV dose over one hour under the same schedule. Both runs use existing membrane parameters. Shorter first dwells are hypothetical.

Vitreous exposure after oral valacyclovir

Huynh et al. collected serum and undiluted vitreous at surgery after four oral valacyclovir doses in ten adults with normal kidney function and noninflamed eyes.

Measured acyclovir at surgery, mean (range)
Serum4.41 (3.18–5.92) mg/L
Vitreous1.03 (0.67–1.33) mg/L
Paired vitreous/serum ratio0.24 (0.16–0.34)

The study gives one sampling occasion per eye. The time course and transfer during dialysis remain open questions. Huynh et al.

Topical acyclovir and skin exposure

Human 5% cream

One hour after the final application, plasma acyclovir was below the assay detection limit of 0.01 µmol/L in five of six volunteers and measured 0.014 µmol/L in the sixth. A separate 20-person study sampled drug in the dermis for 36 hours. Its concentration curves describe local exposure even when little drug reaches plasma. Cream label · Dermal sampling study

Rabbit ointment and iontophoresis

After ointment application, dermal acyclovir was detectable only in the first 30 minutes. With electrically assisted delivery, skin exposure ranged from 11% to 40% of IV-bolus skin exposure across the tested current densities. These animal measurements describe local delivery under the experimental conditions. Human tissue predictions would require a formulation-specific model. Rabbit microdialysis study
