Study design and parameter uncertainty

Each synthetic course represents a 70 kg adult with zero residual creatinine clearance and three one-hour IV infusions at hours 0, 24 and 48. HD runs from hours 28 to 32 and 52 to 56. CAPD uses four 2 L dwells of 4, 4, 4 and 12 hours per day. The second sample tied to dialysis is at the end of the first HD session or at the hour-36 CAPD exchange boundary.

Three off-grid parameter profiles each generate 24 assay-error realizations. Each fit reweights 169 combinations of systemic clearance and dialysis strength. Dialysis strength scales HD clearance or CAPD membrane transport. The assay error has a 5% coefficient of variation with a minimum standard deviation of 0.1 mg/L. The held-out concentration is the noise-free simulated trough.

These results depend on the two parameters varied, the dialysis schedules and the assumed assay error. Coverage describes this simulation and does not establish clinical accuracy or a preferred clinical sampling time.

Experimental design

Each 70 kg adult receives 700 mg of IV acyclovir over one hour at a creatinine clearance of 95 mL/min without dialysis. Measurements at 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8 and 12 hours include simulated Gaussian assay error (5% CV with a minimum SD of 0.1 mg/L).

The fit reweights 441 candidate pairs of clearance and central-volume scale factors. Metabolite formation clearance and other model parameters are fixed. Each example uses the first noise realization for its parameter pair in the seeded recovery experiment. Interval widths describe parameter uncertainty within this model and grid.
