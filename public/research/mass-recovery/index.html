Urinary and peritoneal acyclovir recovery

Stathoulopoulou 2002 — illustrative sensitivity analysis, not patient-matched validation.

Published endpoints

After one 500 mg oral valacyclovir dose, mean 24-hour urinary recovery was 3.4% in four participants with urine output. Mean CAPD recovery was 6.51% across eight participants. These means describe different participant subsets.

The reported denominator is ambiguous: 500 mg tablet mass versus 347.18 mg equimolar acyclovir. Under these interpretations, urinary recovery is 17.00 or 11.80 mg, and CAPD recovery is 32.55 or 22.60 mg. Both are retained; neither interpretation is selected as established.

Illustrative weights 50–90 kg and constant CrCl 0–10 mL/min; one 500 mg dose, bioavailability 0.545, four six-hour 2 L CAPD exchanges with 2.5% glucose and default transport coefficients. Published individual weights, kidney function and exchange prescriptions are unavailable. Endpoint includes the fourth drain. All results use default parameters without fitting.
