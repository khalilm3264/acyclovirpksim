Medical and scientific disclaimer

AyFlux simulates acyclovir and valacyclovir pharmacokinetics for education and exploratory research. It has not been clinically validated for individual dosing, toxicity assessment, or dialysis decisions.

What the concentration curves represent

Plasma, cerebrospinal fluid (CSF), and CMMG values shown by AyFlux are model predictions, not direct patient measurements. The calculations depend on assumed drug distribution, absorption, renal clearance, CSF transfer, metabolite formation, and dialysis parameters. Those assumptions may not apply to an individual patient.

Displayed reference concentrations, antiviral susceptibility values, and reported toxicity associations do not define a safe dose, establish effective treatment, or diagnose toxicity in a specific person.

Uncertainty and validation

Monte Carlo intervals and other uncertainty displays describe variation under a selected set of model assumptions. A displayed 90% interval does not establish 90% clinical predictive accuracy or coverage. Fitting to selected published summary values is not independent patient-level validation.

Hemodialysis, peritoneal dialysis, and CRRT scenarios are simplified and may differ substantially from real treatments, where machine settings, solute clearance, fluid exchange, residual renal function, and patient physiology vary.
