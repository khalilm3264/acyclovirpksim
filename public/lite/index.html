Exact fixed assumptions and limitations

Illustrative adult: 70 kg, residual creatinine clearance 0 mL/min; 700 mg IV acyclovir infused over one hour at hour 0; one dose only; 48-hour modeling horizon; one deterministic parameter set, with no uncertainty band. The dose is an arbitrary software demonstration, not a suggested dose.

Dialysis scenarios: no dialysis; intermittent HD from hours 8 to 12 with modeled clearance 81.8 mL/min; CAPD with four 6-hour, 2-L dwells per day and an exploratory transport model; or CRRT throughout the 48 hours, with effluent 25 mL/kg/h, sieving coefficient 0.95, delivered fraction 0.85. Other PK coefficients follow the nominal full AyFlux model.

All results come from the ordinary AyFlux engine with variability disabled. The comparison cannot represent a patient's drug exposure or account for individual dialysis performance. In particular, PD drug transfer and CMMG handling are exploratory assumptions. Model equations, citations, and limitations.
