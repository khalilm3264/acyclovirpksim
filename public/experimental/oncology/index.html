Acyclovir after chemotherapy

Compare how fast acyclovir peaks and how much exposure accumulates after oral valacyclovir or IV acyclovir.

For pharmacokinetic research and education. The study enrolled neutropenic adults with normal kidney function after high-dose chemotherapy. Use this comparison to examine model behavior.

Oral and IV exposure

Study regimens were 1,000 mg oral valacyclovir or 5 mg/kg IV acyclovir infused over one hour. Both arms received seven doses eight hours apart.

Weight and creatinine clearance are illustrative model inputs. The study summary reports group averages.

Study and model

Höglund and colleagues enrolled 15 participants in a randomized crossover study. Thirteen completed both arms. The measured oral peak was later and lower than the IV peak, with similar eight-hour exposure.

Both oral simulations use AyFlux’s default bioavailability. The experimental curve runs absorption through two sequential stages at the previously selected rate of 1.636 h⁻¹ per stage. The rate is held fixed for this comparison. AyFlux uses the same distribution and clearance equations for all three curves.
