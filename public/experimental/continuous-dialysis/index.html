Published measurements

Collignon et al. measured plasma and effluent in a 21-month-old child receiving continuous venovenous hemodiafiltration (CVVHDF). The six labels below are from Table 1. H2, H5 and H7 come from one sampled infusion; H1, H4 and H8 from a different infusion sampled one day apart. These are not one continuous profile. During sampling, blood flow was 100 mL/min and reported dialysate plus replacement and net fluid removal gave 2.95–2.99 L/h of total effluent.

Show modeled decline using

Illustrative one-compartment decline with no additional input, not a fit to the published curve. Endogenous clearance is fixed at 29.45 mL/min (midpoint of the reported 25.9–33 range); volume is fixed at 10.8 L from the Abdalla population model scaled to 10 kg, not measured in this child. No dialysis prescription or dose recommendation is provided.

Model equation and source details

C(t) = 28 exp[-0.06 (CLendogenous + CLfilter) t / V], where clearance is in mL/min, t is hours after H1, V is in liters, and 0.06 converts mL/min to L/h. Center lines use the midpoint of each method’s range. Only the H4 and H8 prefilter samples from the same sampled infusion as H1 are included as comparison markers, at their nominal offsets. The model does not estimate patient-specific volume, filter adsorption, or dosing history. No medians or confidence limits are inferred from the reported ranges.
