Oral absorption comparison

How does absorption change the time of the plasma acyclovir peak after a single 500 mg dose of valacyclovir? Compare three absorption models with concentrations digitized from published group means.

Model total AUC equals absorbed acyclovir mass divided by clearance, so all three absorption models give the same total within a study group. Fit and evidence

The baseline has one absorption stage (1.8 h⁻¹). The lag model delays its start by 0.590 h. The two-stage model passes the absorbed dose through two successive stages at 1.636 h⁻¹. Both candidates were developed on Weller. An independent numerical refit to the ten digitized Weller points gives 0.589 h and 1.635 h⁻¹. Both Phan groups use the original frozen candidates.

All curves use the same 54.5% bioavailability and the adult disposition parameters from AyFlux revision 8fc1c9d. These group-level comparisons cover a single dose in adults without dialysis. They cannot establish individual dose requirements.
